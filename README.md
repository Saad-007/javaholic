# Javaholics: Fairview's Rhythm

Build a modern, premium single-page website for "Javaholics" — a specialty coffee 

shop with a tech-house/music culture identity in Fairview, Dublin. This should feel 

sophisticated, minimal, and slightly underground — specialty coffee during the day 

with a subtle music/tech-house influence. NOT a nightclub website, NOT a generic 

traditional café template. Think polished, modern, premium.

BUSINESS DETAILS

═══════════════════════════════════

- Name: Javaholics

- Location: 1B Merville Ave, Fairview, Dublin, D03 A9W0, Ireland

- Phone: +353 1 818 6747

- Hours: Open daily, closes 6 PM

- Rating: 4.5★ (415+ Google reviews)

- Price range: €1-10 per person

- Signature items: Michelle Java, Roadworks Coffee of the Day, Flat White, 

  Americano w Hot Milk, Full Irish Breakfast, Frozen Coffee, Large Cappuccino, 

  Fresh Fruit Pancakes, Blueberry Muffin, Carrot Cake with Americano

- What customers say: "the best coffee in D3," "absolute favourite cafe in Dublin," 

  warm and cozy atmosphere, overlooks beautiful views of Fairview, outdoor seating, 

  guitars on the wall, friendly and attentive staff (Ayca specifically mentioned)

- Identity: specialty coffee + tech-house/music culture — the shop has guitars, 

  artwork, and music-related decor as part of its personality. Keep the character 

  Javaholics already has, just make it look polished and modern online.

DESIGN SYSTEM (CLIENT-SPECIFIED — FOLLOW EXACTLY)

═══════════════════════════════════

Color Palette:

- Primary background: dark charcoal / near-black (#161616 or #1A1A1A)

- Secondary background (alternating sections): warm cream / off-white (#F5F0E8 

  or #FAF6EF)

- Accent: warm gold / caramel (#C9974D or #D4A857) — used sparingly for highlights, 

  buttons, dividers, small details

- Natural wood and coffee tones should come through in the photography itself 

  (warm browns, tans) rather than as flat UI color blocks

- Text: cream/off-white on dark sections, charcoal on cream sections

Typography:

- Headings: clean, modern, slightly condensed sans-serif with a premium feel 

  (e.g. "Neue Montreal", "General Sans", or "Suisse Int'l") — confident but not 

  loud, minimal letter-spacing adjustments for elegance

- Body: same font family family or a clean pairing, highly readable

- Avoid anything playful, rounded, or "coffee shop cute" — this needs to feel 

  premium and slightly underground, more like a specialty record label or design 

  studio than a café

Layout & Mood:

- Clean, minimal, generous white/black space — not cluttered

- Strong, high-quality photography is the hero of this site — real photos of the 

  shop exterior, interior, coffee, food, the coffee machine/bar area, and music 

  details/decor (guitars, artwork) should be used prominently, large and full-bleed 

  where possible

- Charcoal black and cream white should alternate between sections for rhythm, 

  with gold used only as a subtle accent (thin lines, small icons, button borders)

- Subtle grain/texture overlay optional for a slightly underground, editorial feel

- Rounded corners should be minimal/small if used at all — this leans more 

  architectural/sharp than soft and bubbly

Micro-interactions:

- Subtle fade-up animations on scroll — smooth, understated, never bouncy or playful

- Images: slow, subtle zoom on hover

- Buttons: thin gold border, fills or underlines smoothly on hover

- Sticky header: dark, minimal, gains a subtle blur on scroll
